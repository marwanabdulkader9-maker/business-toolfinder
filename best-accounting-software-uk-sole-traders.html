Best accounting software for UK sole traders

Choosing accounting software can save time, but the right option depends on your business, budget and the features you actually need.

What should a sole trader look for?

Sole traders often need straightforward software rather than a complicated finance system. Before choosing a product, consider whether it provides the features you actually need.

  • Easy income and expense tracking
  • Professional invoices
  • Receipt and document management
  • Bank transaction integration
  • Useful reports
  • Tax-related record keeping
  • Mobile access
  • Responsive customer support

Free vs paid accounting software

Free plans can be useful when starting out, but they may have limitations on invoices, users, integrations or reporting.

Paid software can provide more features and automation. However, the cheapest option isn't necessarily the best value if it lacks something your business actually needs.

How to choose

Make a shortlist of the features that matter to you first. Then compare the total cost, ease of use, integrations, support and contract terms.

If you're unsure, start with the smallest plan that genuinely meets your needs rather than paying for features you won't use.
