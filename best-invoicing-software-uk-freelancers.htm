Best invoicing software for UK freelancers

Good invoicing software can make it easier to create professional invoices, keep track of payments and reduce repetitive administration.

What should freelancers look for?

Freelancers often want invoicing software that is quick to set up and doesn't add unnecessary complexity. Look for features that fit the way you actually work.

  • Professional invoice templates
  • Automatic invoice numbering
  • Recurring invoices where appropriate
  • Payment tracking
  • Payment reminders
  • Expense recording
  • Mobile access
  • Accounting integrations

Free vs paid invoicing software

Some services offer free invoicing features, which can be useful when you're starting out. However, free plans may limit the number of invoices, clients, integrations or payment features available.

Paid plans can make sense when invoicing becomes a regular part of your business administration.

Don't forget payment tracking

Creating an invoice is only part of the process. If you're regularly chasing unpaid invoices, look for software that can help you monitor outstanding balances and send reminders.

Check whether payment features have additional transaction charges before choosing a provider.

How to choose

Start by deciding what you actually need: simple invoices, payment collection, expense tracking, accounting integration, recurring billing or something more advanced.

Compare the total cost rather than looking only at the advertised monthly subscription.
