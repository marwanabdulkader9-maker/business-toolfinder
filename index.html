Frequently asked questions

A few things you should know before using the site.

Does BusinessToolFinder sell software?

No. We help visitors discover software and provide links to providers. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by the relevant provider.

Do you receive commission?

Some links may be affiliate or referral links. Where this applies, we aim to make the commercial relationship clear.

Is this financial or accounting advice?

No. BusinessToolFinder is a software-discovery website. The information provided is general information and is not financial, tax, legal or accounting advice.

How should I choose business software?

Consider price, features, integrations, ease of use, customer support, contract terms and whether the product actually meets your business requirements.

Do I pay extra when using an affiliate link?

Generally, affiliate commissions are paid by the provider or affiliate programme rather than being added to the customer's price. Always check the provider's current terms and pricing.

Privacy
